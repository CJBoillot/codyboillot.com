Cozy Zone Collector

Finish Azeroth one zone at a time.

An addon for World of Warcraft (Retail, Midnight) that turns every zone into a checklist you can actually finish. Open any of 169 zones across 15 continents and see one completion number, then exactly what's left: quests, rares, treasures, exploration, flight paths, achievements, battle pets, recipes, transmog appearances and housing decor. Switch between your whole account and the character you're playing for a one-character 100% run, and anything that character can't do (another class, the other faction, a holiday, a removed quest) is set aside with the reason shown, so the number you chase is one you can reach.

Alliance and Horde zones are tinted royal blue and Horde red, the window can follow you as you travel, and a drag-to-hotbar button opens it in one click. Zone data is extracted once from AllTheThings (MIT) and bundled, so it needs no other addon to run.
