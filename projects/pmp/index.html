PMP Rapid-Fire: a study app for the 2026 PMP exam

A rapid-fire way to learn the terms and principles behind the PMP exam that changed in July 2026. Study 20 flashcards, take a 20-question quiz on the same cards, then review the grade. Wrong answers go on a missed list to retake later.

667 cards, every one with an explanation. Terms, the principle behind each task in PMI's 2026 exam outline, formulas, commonly confused pairs and PMI's decision rules, built from a 1,656-question bank and a 490-term glossary with duplicates merged and errors fixed.

Study, quiz, review. 42 themed sets of 20 keep related ideas together, and a quiz builder makes a set from any mix of sections, a domain or a focus topic. Quizzes are graded only at the end, like the real exam, and 1,630 exam-style scenario questions test each principle the way the exam asks it.

Progress that follows you. Cards covered, accuracy by domain and theme, and a full answer history save in the browser, and sync between phone and laptop with Google sign-in.
