Why I teach it this way

I've hired and trained more than 70 engineers. The steps above follow the same habits I coach on my teams.

  • Start with the outcome. Decide what the site must prove before you open a tool.
  • Direct the AI. Give it context, constraints and one clear ask. Don't just accept the first answer.
  • Verify before it ships. You own every word on your site, whoever typed it.
  • Ask for honest feedback. Find the weak spots before an employer does.
