WoW Forever PvP Planner

Pick your duo before the queue pops.

Built for the launch of World of Warcraft: Forever, the PvP Planner grades every one of the 406 possible two-spec pairings across all 28 specializations on Fun, Effectiveness and Ease, each 1 to 5, for a color-coded total out of 15. Every pairing carries a one-sentence read on why it scores the way it does, grounded in the actual kit: Shatter combos, Pain Suppression, Windfury, Viper Sting, Fear.

Filter each player by role (Healer, Tank, Melee, Ranged) or exact spec, sort by any axis, and the Top Picks section recalculates for whatever you have selected. A Solo mode grades each spec alone on Fun, Ease, Leveling and solo PvP out of 20. Save the combos you are weighing, then copy them as a ready-made prompt to paste into an AI for a deeper write-up. A Races & Racials sheet covers both factions with the latest pre-launch racial tuning.
