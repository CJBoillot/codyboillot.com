WoW Forever PvP Planner

Pick your duo before the queue pops.

Built for the launch of World of Warcraft: Forever, the PvP Planner grades every one of the 406 possible two-spec pairings across all 28 specializations on Fun, Effectiveness and Ease, each 1 to 5, for a color-coded total out of 15. Every pairing carries a one-sentence read on why it scores the way it does, grounded in the actual kit: Shatter combos, Pain Suppression, Windfury, Viper Sting, Fear.

Every spec has its own portrait, and every grade is a shield badge with a five-segment bar, colored by the grade alone: A blue, B green, C yellow, D red, F gray. Filter each player by role (Healer, Tank, Melee, Ranged) or exact spec, sort by any axis, and the Top Picks section recalculates for whatever you have selected. A Solo mode grades each spec alone on Fun, Ease, Leveling and solo PvP out of 20. Save the combos you are weighing, then copy them as a ready-made prompt to paste into an AI for a deeper write-up. A Race & Class Explorer covers every Forever race and class: racial traits with the latest pre-launch tuning, every race's playable classes, and all 27 specializations with their roles.
