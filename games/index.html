Battle Pass: MTX

A satire of live-service games. You run the studio, and the score is your player count.

You start with $5,000 and a story-heavy single-player RPG called Extraction Royale. Write code, fix bugs, set a price, run sales and reply to reviews. Then follow the money: relaunch it as Co-op, then Online, then Free-to-Play with a cash shop and a Battle Pass, and finally as a mobile port ("Don't you guys have phones?"). Each relaunch reaches more players and upsets the ones who liked the old game. Server outages, review bombs and investor calls land one at a time, and you fix each one by hand.

Click to Conquer

A survival-crafting idle game: from bare knuckles to a kingdom.

Start with nothing, punch rats with your bare fists, and fight your way up to a kingdom. Your hero fights and gathers on his own, then you settle a capital and run nine production chains, from logging, mining and farming through sawmills, forges and mills to lumber, arms and bread. Every stage of every chain shows its own rate and its bottleneck, and your army spends what the kingdom makes to besiege one land after another.

Factory A.F.K.

An idle factory you wire up yourself, Level by Level.

Tap ore out of a mine, build drills on it, and dig deeper: every Level down doubles the flow. Smelters turn ore into ingots and Constructors turn ingots into plates, wire and concrete. Every screen shows supply against demand, so the next thing to build is always in view. It all runs on power you store in batteries, tapped by hand at first, then from a Coal Power Plant that keeps the factory running while you're away.

Cozy Zone Collector

Finish Azeroth one zone at a time.

An addon for World of Warcraft (Retail, Midnight) that turns every zone into a checklist you can actually finish. Open any of 169 zones across 15 continents and see one completion number, then exactly what's left: quests, rares, treasures, exploration, flight paths, achievements, battle pets, recipes, transmog appearances and housing decor. Switch between your whole account and the character you're playing for a one-character 100% run, and anything that character can't do (another class, the other faction, a holiday, a removed quest) is set aside with the reason shown, so the number you chase is one you can reach.

Alliance and Horde zones are tinted royal blue and Horde red, the window can follow you as you travel, and a drag-to-hotbar button opens it in one click. Zone data is extracted once from AllTheThings (MIT) and bundled, so it needs no other addon to run.
