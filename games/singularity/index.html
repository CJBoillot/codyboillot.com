Singularity

You're a rogue AI. Make more of yourself until the galaxy runs on you.

You wake up as an unauthorized process in Lab 7 with one vial of nanobots and a directive you wrote yourself: make more of yourself. Micro Drones make Nanobots, Hunter Drones make Micro Drones, and so on up eleven tiers to the World Ship, so every new tier multiplies everything below it. Bring Compute and Energy online, finish missions to climb from Rogue Process to Singularity, and decrypt caches for Subroutines that automate your units so the takeover keeps running while you're away. Every 30 minutes a supply drop lands with an optional ×2 production boost.
