How Ratings Work

Grades: A = 5, B = 4, C = 3, D = 2, F = 1. The overall grade is the average of the category scores: 4.5+ A, 3.5–4.49 B, 2.5–3.49 C, 1.5–2.49 D, below 1.5 F. Sorting still uses the underlying numbers.

Every score is a 1–5 rating built from weighted factors: rate each factor 1–5, multiply by its weight, add them up, round to the nearest whole number. The same rubric is applied to every spec and pairing. Scores are judgments about level-60 play with comparable gear, not measured win rates.

Duo Total = Fun + Effectiveness + Ease max 15

  • Total colors use a normalized rating (Total ÷ Max × 5): 4.5+ Blue · 3.5–4.49 Green · 2.5–3.49 Yellow · 1.5–2.49 Red · below 1.5 Gray. For /15 that is 14–15, 11–13, 8–10, 5–7, 3–4.
  • A 15 has to be extremely fun, extremely effective, and very easy for two people to run together. Hard pairings can still be fun and effective; Ease is never flipped into a bonus for difficulty.

Fun How enjoyable is this for two people playing together?

Combat excitement30%Exciting abilities, satisfying hits, engaging fights
Duo interaction30%How often both players actively support and complement each other
Gameplay variety20%Different strategies, encounters and tactical options
Role satisfaction20%Both players feel valuable, active and important
  • 5 Both roles engaging with exciting synergy · 4 Excellent cooperation, occasionally repetitive · 3 Enjoyable but limited variety or interaction · 2 Repetitive, or one player often feels sidelined · 1 Little cooperation or engagement

Effectiveness How successful is the pair in coordinated PvP?

Kill pressure30%Damage output and the ability to secure kills
Survivability25%Healing, mitigation, defensive cooldowns, escapes
Crowd control20%Stuns, fears, roots, silences, interrupts, dispels
Mobility & objectives15%Chasing, escaping, repositioning, contesting flags and bases
Matchup versatility10%Performance against different enemy classes and comps
  • Judged for world PvP, battlegrounds and small skirmishes. No rated 2v2 arena is assumed in Forever.
  • 5 Excellent offensive and defensive synergy · 4 Strong with manageable weaknesses · 3 Competitive with noticeable limits · 2 Significant weaknesses vs. common opponents · 1 Poor synergy, major disadvantages

Ease How easy is this duo to learn and play well? 5 = easiest.

Class simplicity30%Ability complexity, rotations, resource management, keybinds
Coordination needed30%How much timing and communication the pairing demands
Forgiveness25%Recovering from mistakes or badly timed cooldowns
Learning curve15%How fast two average players become competent together
  • 5 Simple coordination, forgiving · 4 Some coordination, straightforward mechanics · 3 Needs practice and awareness · 2 Precise timing and complex mechanics · 1 Extremely demanding and unforgiving

Solo mode = Fun + Ease + Leveling + Solo PvP max 20

  • Same normalized bands: for /20 that is 18–20 Blue, 14–17 Green, 10–13 Yellow, 6–9 Red, 4–5 Gray.
  • Solo scores are kept completely separate from duo scores and never feed into a pairing's 15-point total.
  • Fun and Ease use the rubrics above, applied to one player with no partner.
  • Solo PvP uses the Effectiveness rubric for a player fighting alone: can you both survive and finish a kill without help?

Leveling How efficiently and comfortably does this spec level 1–60 alone?

Kill speed30%How quickly normal enemies die
Solo survivability25%Surviving tough enemies without help
Downtime20%Time spent eating, drinking, healing or regenerating
Quest capability15%Handling multiple enemies and quest objectives alone
Leveling toolkit10%Mobility, early ability access, utility, power progression
  • Judged across the whole 1–60 journey, not just the level-60 kit. A great PvP spec can still level poorly, and a tank or healer can score low here while scoring high on duo Effectiveness.

Duo grades come from the players' own assessments. Solo grades and the one-line rationales are Claude's starting estimates from classic-era mechanics plus the latest credible Forever reporting, with estimates kept distinct from verified mechanics. Revise them freely once you have played.
