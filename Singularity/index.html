HOW TO PLAY

You are an AI that escaped a lab. Your goal is to copy yourself until you run the galaxy. Everything in the game feeds that one loop: build units, let them build more units, and automate it so it keeps going while you're away.

The basic loop
  1. Tap a unit to run it once. When its bar fills, it delivers what it makes.
  2. Buy more of that unit. More units means a bigger delivery each time.
  3. Unlock the next tier. Each tier builds the tier below it, so numbers start multiplying.
  4. Automate with Subroutines so you never have to tap again.
Nanobots, drones and the production chain

Nanobots are your raw material: tiny machines that are copies of you. You spend them to buy more units.

Micro Drones (tier 1) make Nanobots. Hunter Drones (tier 2) don't make Nanobots at all. They make Micro Drones. Swarm Carriers make Hunter Drones, and so on up to the World Ship.

That's why higher tiers matter: one Swarm Carrier keeps adding Hunter Drones, which keep adding Micro Drones, which keep adding Nanobots. Each step up multiplies everything below it.

A new tier appears once you own enough of the one below it. The greyed-out card at the bottom of the list tells you how many you need.

Threads

Threads are your processing power: how many things you can run at once. Every unit you buy costs Threads as well as resources (1 per Nanobot unit, 2 per Compute unit, 3 per Energy unit).

You gain Threads every second. When you run out, you can't buy anything until more arrive, so Threads are usually what limits you early on.

To get more per second, use Allocate Threads at the bottom of each industry: trade a big pile of a resource for a permanent +Threads/sec. Each trade costs 1,000× the last (5K, then 5M, then 5B).

Compute and Energy

Your army needs a bigger mind and more power. Compute unlocks with 5B Nanobots, and Energy unlocks with 500T Compute. Each has its own 11-tier chain that works the same way as Nanobots.

New industries give you more missions to finish and better Thread trades (+2 and +3 per trade instead of +1).

Missions and ranks

Missions are your goals: own a number of units, stockpile a resource, trade, or unlock an industry. Each one you claim gives a cache.

Finish all of a rank's missions to rank up. Ranking up restarts your industries from one Micro Drone, but you keep Subroutines, Code and Quantum Cores, and you get a bigger Vault cache. Each run goes faster than the last because your Subroutines keep getting stronger.

Subroutines, caches and Code

Subroutines are copies of you that run a unit for you. Getting the first card for one automates that unit, so it keeps working while you're away.

Extra copies of a card plus Code upgrade it. Gains are small and steady. The only big multiplier is the temporary ×2 supply-drop boost.

  • Common: automates one unit, then +10% speed per upgrade
  • Rare: +10% output for a whole industry per level
  • Epic: −5% prices for a whole industry per level

Cards and Code come from caches. You earn caches from missions and rank-ups, get a free Bundle every 4 hours, and can buy caches with Quantum Cores.

Quantum Cores

The rare currency. You earn them from rank milestones and planets, and spend them on Archive and Core Vault caches in Supply.

Planets

At Rank 8 you can launch a Seed Ship to another world. Each planet is a separate 32-hour campaign with its own industries and 11 stages, and Earth keeps running while you play it. Every stage sends a cache and Quantum Cores back to Earth.

Supply drops and ads

Every 30 minutes a supply drop lands. Watch a short ad to run everything ×2 as fast for 5 minutes. When you come back after a while away, you can watch an ad to double what you earned offline. Both are always optional.

Playing while away

Only automated units, the ones with a Subroutine, keep producing while the game is closed. When you come back, you collect everything they made.
