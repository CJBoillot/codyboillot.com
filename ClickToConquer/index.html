Bestiary

The more of a creature you kill, the better you fight it: Familiar 10 kills (+2%), Studied 50 (+5%), Known 200 (+10%), Mastered 1,000 (+15%) damage dealt and less taken against that kind. Kill counts survive founding.

Trophies

Kill milestones for every kind of enemy: Bronze 1,000 kills · Silver 3,000 · Gold 10,000 · Platinum 30,000 · Diamond 100,000. Every trophy you earn gives +2% loot and XP, forever. Trophies are never lost.
